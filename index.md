# Privacy Policy for HomeoRemedy

**Last Updated:** September 26, 2026

## Introduction

HomeoRemedy ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our mobile application, including our AI-powered feature, **Ask AI**.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Account Information

If you create an account (email/password or Google Sign-In), we collect:

- **Email address and display name**
- A **device identifier**, used only to enforce a one-per-device free-trial limit and to prevent trial abuse

### 2. Ask AI Consultation Data

Ask AI lets you describe symptoms in your own words so the app can suggest relevant information. When you use this feature, we collect and store:

- The **symptoms, answers, and messages** you type or speak into a consultation
- The AI's responses and any resulting case summary

This is health-related information. Here is exactly what happens to it:

- Your messages are sent to **Anthropic's Claude AI model, accessed through Amazon Web Services (AWS) Bedrock**, in order to generate a response. Anthropic and AWS process this content to serve your request; per AWS Bedrock's terms, it is not used to train Anthropic's or Amazon's underlying models.
- This processing can occur in AWS/Anthropic infrastructure **outside your own country**, including outside India. See "International Data Transfers" below.
- Your full consultation (messages and responses) is **stored on our servers indefinitely**, tied to your account, so you can revisit past consultations from "Case History." It is **not** automatically deleted.
- You can delete an individual consultation, all of your consultation history, or your entire account at any time — see "Your Rights and Choices" below. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

**By using Ask AI, you consent to your symptom information being processed as described above.** If you are not comfortable with this, please do not use the Ask AI feature — the rest of the app (the remedy library, remedy finder, saved items) does not require it.

### 3. Purchases (AI Credits)

Ask AI consultations are paid for using credits, purchasable in-app.

- Purchases are processed by **Google Play Billing**, via **RevenueCat**.
- We do **not** receive or store your card, UPI, or other payment instrument details.
- We keep a record of each transaction (amount, date, credits granted) for accounting and tax purposes, for as long as the law requires.

### 4. Advertising

Free-tier users see ads served through **Google AdMob** (native ads, app-open ads, and rewarded ads). Paid subscribers do not see ads.

- AdMob collects advertising identifiers and ad-interaction data on your device in order to select, serve, and measure ads, per Google's own privacy policy.
- Ads served to users in India and other regions outside the EU/UK are **not** restricted to "non-personalized" — they may be personalized based on Google's advertising data.
- We are still implementing region-specific ad-consent handling (Google's User Messaging Platform) for EEA/UK users; if this affects you, treat ads as personalized until this is completed.

### 5. Automatically Collected Information (Firebase Analytics)

We use **Firebase Analytics** (provided by Google) to collect anonymous usage data:

- **Device Information:** Device model, operating system version, device identifiers
- **Usage Data:** Screens viewed, features used, buttons clicked, time spent in app
- **Geographic Information:** Country/region level only (not precise location)
- **App Performance Data:** Crash reports, error logs, app response times

### Information We DO NOT Collect

- ❌ Precise GPS location
- ❌ Photos, contacts, or other device data unrelated to the app
- ❌ Your card, UPI, or bank details (purchases are handled entirely by Google Play Billing)

## How We Use Information

- **Provide Ask AI:** Generate consultation responses and let you revisit past consultations
- **Process Purchases:** Grant the correct AI-credit balance for what you bought
- **Prevent Abuse:** Enforce the one-time free trial per person/device
- **Improve App Functionality:** Understand which features are most useful
- **Fix Bugs:** Identify and resolve technical issues and crashes
- **Serve Ads:** To free-tier users, via Google AdMob
- **Monitor Performance:** Ensure the app runs smoothly on different devices

## Data Sharing and Third Parties

| Service | Purpose | What it receives |
|---|---|---|
| **Firebase Authentication (Google)** | Account creation / sign-in | Email, display name |
| **Anthropic Claude, via AWS Bedrock** | Generates Ask AI responses | The content of your consultation messages |
| **Amazon Web Services (AWS)** | Hosts our backend and database | Your account ID and consultation history |
| **RevenueCat / Google Play Billing** | Processes credit purchases | Purchase/transaction data (not your card details) |
| **Google AdMob** | Serves ads to free-tier users | Advertising identifiers, ad interaction data |
| **Firebase Analytics (Google)** | Anonymous usage analytics | Device info, usage events, crash data |

### What We Do NOT Do

- ❌ We do NOT sell your data to third parties
- ❌ We do NOT provide data to data brokers
- ❌ We do NOT use your Ask AI consultation content for advertising or marketing

## Data Retention

- **Ask AI consultations:** Retained indefinitely, tied to your account, until you delete them (individually, in bulk, or via account deletion)
- **Analytics Data:** Retained for 14 months, then automatically deleted by Firebase
- **Purchase records:** Retained as long as required by tax/accounting law
- **App Preferences:** Stored locally on your device only

## Your Rights and Choices

### Right to Access
You can request information about what data we have collected by emailing us.

### Right to Deletion
- Delete a single consultation or your entire case history from the **Case History** tab — takes effect immediately.
- Delete your entire account (account, consultations, credits, saved remedies) from **Settings → Delete Account**, or by emailing us if you cannot sign in.
- Full details: see our [Account Deletion page](./delete-account).

### Opt-Out Options
- **Analytics:** Email us to opt out of analytics tracking (we do not currently offer an in-app toggle for this).
- **Ask AI:** Simply don't use the Ask AI feature — no account or consultation data is created unless you do.
- **Uninstall App:** Removes local data from your device, but does **not** delete your account or server-stored consultations — see the Account Deletion page.

### How to Exercise Your Rights
Email us at: **homeoremedyofficial@gmail.com**
We will respond within 7 business days.

## Special Notice for EEA/UK/California/Washington Users

Health-related information (such as what you share with Ask AI) is treated as a special/sensitive category of data under laws including the EU/UK GDPR, and as "consumer health data" under laws such as Washington's My Health My Data Act. If you are located in one of these places:

- We rely on your **explicit consent** (given by choosing to use Ask AI, after reading this notice) as the basis for processing your consultation content.
- Your data is transferred internationally, to AWS/Anthropic infrastructure, as described above.
- You may withdraw consent at any time by ceasing to use Ask AI and deleting your existing consultations as described in "Your Rights and Choices."
- You may still see personalized ads via Google AdMob; see "Advertising" above.
