# Privacy Policy for HomeoRemedy

**Last Updated:** March 21, 2026

## Introduction

HomeoRemedy ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Automatically Collected Information

We use **Firebase Analytics** (provided by Google) to collect anonymous usage data:

- **Device Information:** Device model, operating system version, device identifiers
- **Usage Data:** Screens viewed, features used, buttons clicked, time spent in app
- **Geographic Information:** Country/region level only (not precise location)
- **App Performance Data:** Crash reports, error logs, app response times

### 2. Information We DO NOT Collect

We want to be clear about what we do NOT collect:

- ❌ Personal identifying information (name, email, phone number, address)
- ❌ Medical information or health data
- ❌ Search queries or symptom selections
- ❌ Remedy searches or treatment interests
- ❌ User-generated content or notes
- ❌ Precise location data (GPS coordinates)
- ❌ Photos, contacts, or other device data
- ❌ Payment information (app is free)

## How We Use Information

We use collected data solely to:

- **Improve App Functionality:** Understand which features are most useful
- **Fix Bugs:** Identify and resolve technical issues and crashes
- **Enhance User Experience:** Optimize navigation and interface design
- **Develop New Features:** Understand user needs and preferences
- **Monitor Performance:** Ensure app runs smoothly on different devices
