# Delete your HomeoRemedy account

**Last Updated:** September 16, 2026

This page explains how to permanently delete your **HomeoRemedy** account and the data
associated with it.

## Option 1: Delete it yourself, inside the app

This is the fastest route and takes effect immediately. No email or waiting period is involved.

1. Open **HomeoRemedy** and make sure you are signed in.
2. Go to the **Settings** tab.
3. Tap **Delete account**.
4. Tap **Continue** on the first confirmation.
5. Tap **Delete permanently** on the second confirmation.

Your account and data are erased right away and you are returned to the signed-out screen.
There are two confirmation steps because this cannot be undone.

## Option 2: If you cannot sign in

If you have lost access to your account and cannot reach the Settings screen, email
**homeoremedyofficial@gmail.com** from the email address you registered with, using the subject
line *Account deletion request*.

We verify that the request comes from the registered address and then delete the account on your
behalf. Please allow up to **30 days**, though it is usually completed much sooner.

## Deleting only your consultation history, and keeping your account

You do not have to delete your whole account to remove your health information. Inside the app:

- Open the **Case History** tab.
- To remove one consultation, swipe or tap the delete control on that entry.
- To remove all of them at once, tap **Clear All**.

Either action deletes the consultation from our servers immediately, including the symptoms you
described and the AI conversation. Your account, your remaining credits and your saved remedies
are untouched. This cannot be undone.

## What is deleted when you delete your account

All of the following is erased permanently and cannot be recovered:

- **Your account and sign-in credentials** — your email address, name and profile details are
  removed, and the sign-in account itself is destroyed.
- **Your consultations and case history** — including everything you described about your
  symptoms, and every AI conversation.
- **Remaining AI credits** — any unused credits, including purchased ones, are forfeited and are
  not refunded.
- **Saved remedies and preferences** — along with your app settings and language choice.

## What is kept, and why

Two things deliberately outlive the account. Neither can identify you, and neither is used for
advertising or shared with anyone.

**A one-way marker that the free trial was already used.** The free trial is offered once per
person and once per device. To enforce that after deletion, we keep an irreversible
`HMAC-SHA256` fingerprint of your email address and of your device identifier. A fingerprint
cannot be reversed back into an email address or a device ID — it can only be compared against a
future signup. This is why creating a new account will not restore free trial credits.

**Purchase records required by law.** If you bought credits, the transaction record is retained
for tax and accounting purposes for as long as the law requires. Purchases are processed by
Google Play; we never receive or store your card or payment details.

## Deleting the app is not the same thing

Uninstalling HomeoRemedy removes it from your phone but leaves your account and your
consultations on our servers. Use one of the two options above to delete the account itself.
